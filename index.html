About

Thermal transport, atomistic simulation, and AI4X workflows.

I am a postdoctoral researcher in Electronic Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong. My work focuses on scalable simulations, machine-learning potentials, and reproducible computational tools for functional materials.

At CUHK, I work with Prof. Jianbin Xu on atomistic modeling and thermal transport in advanced materials.
